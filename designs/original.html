Fast, by design.

Less time moving data. More time doing the work. W is designed to eliminate intermediate objects, fuse operations, and specialize hot paths.

Travel light.

No garbage collector or conventional heap. W reuses temporary storage and constructs results where they belong, keeping the footprint small.
